# Refunds

Refunds are issued from the dashboard. A refund moves money out of your
account, and only your passkey can authorise that, so an API key cannot
complete one. Your server can [preview](/api#refunds) what a refund would
send.

<Steps>

### Open the payment

In [app.keycat.net](https://app.keycat.net), open your business and choose the
payment under **Payments**, then enter the amount and an optional reason. A
late or duplicate payment under **Needs attention** opens the same screen.
Each refund is for one payment: partial refunds add up, up to what that
payment was, so on a reusable link one customer cannot be refunded what
another paid.

The screen shows where the refund goes, in full. When your customer paid,
checkout asked where they were paying from: from a wallet app, a refund goes
back to the address that sent the payment; from an exchange account, it goes
to the deposit address they entered.

### Review it

The review shows the amount, the customer's address, the coin and network,
the cost of sending it, and what your customer receives. A payment made in
USDC on Base is refunded in USDC on Base. A payment made in anything else is
converted back to that coin on that network on the way.

### Confirm with your passkey

Confirming signs one transfer from your account, and the refund leaves then.
There is no link to pass on and nothing for the customer to collect.

</Steps>

Your customer bears the cost of sending a refund, as they bear every fee: the
network fee comes out of what is sent, and a conversion's cost comes out of
what arrives. A refund no larger than the cost of sending it is refused.

When the refund reaches your customer, your webhook receives
`refund.succeeded`, with `amount` (what you refunded), `paid_amount` (what left
after the cost of sending), `destination` and `transaction_hash`. A refund
paid in USDC on Base is done when your transfer confirms; a converted one is
done when the conversion delivers, usually within a minute. If a conversion
cannot be delivered, the money comes back to your account and your webhook
receives `refund.failed`.

<Callout>
  Card payments, and payments made before checkout asked where customers pay
  from, cannot be refunded through Keycat, because there is no address to send
  them to. Arrange those refunds with your customer directly.
</Callout>
