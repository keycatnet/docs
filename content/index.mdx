# Keycat

Keycat takes payments for your business. A customer pays by card, or with a
coin they already hold, from any wallet or exchange on a supported network.
You receive USDC on Base, in an account that only your passkey can move money
out of.

## How it fits together

1. You create a charge from your server, or share a payment link or your
   payment page. Each one has a hosted checkout on `pay.keycat.net`.
2. The customer pays by card, or in the coin they hold, on the network they
   hold it on. Checkout shows the full price, fees included, before they pay.
3. USDC arrives in your business account on Base. You receive the full
   invoice amount; the payer covers the fees.
4. Your server hears about it through a signed webhook, and can replay every
   event at any time.

## What to expect

Your business account is a [Safe](/protocol/account) smart account on Base,
and its owner is your passkey. Keycat holds no key that can move money out of
it: every withdrawal and refund needs your signature.

You receive 100% of the invoice. Fees are added to what the customer pays and
never taken from what you receive. See [fees](/payments#fees).

A payment that has settled to your account is final, a card payment included.
The one exception is a reorganisation of the Base network, which Keycat
reports as [`charge.reversed`](/webhooks#chargereversed).

You never need ETH. The network fee for moving money out of your account is
paid in USDC from that account.

For the contracts, signatures and permissions behind all of this, read
[How Keycat works](/protocol).
