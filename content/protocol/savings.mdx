# Savings

With savings on, Keycat keeps part of your balance available and lends the
rest to the [Aave V3](https://aave.com) USDC market on Base, where it earns
the market's supply rate. Your Safe holds the position as aUSDC. Only an
owner's signature brings it back.

## The permission

Savings works through a [Zodiac Roles](https://github.com/gnosisguild/zodiac-modifier-roles)
v2.1.1 modifier, one per business, owned by your Safe.

- **Deployed for your Safe alone.** The modifier is a minimal proxy of the
  Roles mastercopy, deployed through Zodiac's ModuleProxyFactory with
  `setUp(owner, avatar, target)` all set to your Safe and salt nonce 0. Its
  address is a function of your Safe's address, so you can compute it.
- **Enabled as a module of your Safe.** Turning savings on is one batch your
  passkey signs: deploy the modifier, `enableModule` it, scope the role below,
  and assign that role to Keycat's savings operator.
- **Scoped to two calls.** The role can reach exactly two functions, each with
  its parameters pinned:

```text
Aave Pool  supply(asset, amount, onBehalfOf, referralCode)
             asset         == USDC
             amount        any
             onBehalfOf    == your Safe
             referralCode  == 0

USDC       approve(spender, amount)
             spender       == Aave Pool
             amount        any
```

No other target and no other function is scoped, and the role carries no
permission to send value or delegatecall. In particular `Pool.withdraw` and
`USDC.transfer` are not in it. The modifier's owner is your Safe, so only your
passkey can change the scope.

The amount is deliberately not capped on chain. The worst a stolen savings
operator key can do is move your available USDC into your own Aave position,
which your passkey can withdraw.

## When Keycat supplies

Every fifteen minutes, and right after you save your savings settings, Keycat
reviews each business with savings on, one at a time. It supplies only when
all of these hold:

- the modifier is still enabled on your Safe;
- Aave's USDC utilisation on Base is below your pause threshold (90 to 95%,
  default 95%), because new supply into a market that is nearly fully borrowed
  may be slow to come back;
- your available balance is above your buffer, 25 to 30% of your whole
  balance (available plus saved);
- the surplus is at least $0.10, and no more than a per-move cap.

If Aave's allowance from your Safe is short, the operator first approves
exactly the shortfall, as its own operation, then supplies. Every skipped
review records why (`below_minimum`, `buffer_not_exceeded`,
`supply_paused_for_liquidity`, `approval_pending` or
`utilization_unavailable`), and the dashboard shows the reason.

The operator pays the gas for these moves from its own account. They cost you
nothing.

## Taking money back

Money in savings comes back only through operations your passkey signs:

- **Make more available** calls `Pool.withdraw(USDC, amount, safe)`.
- **Send** and **Refund** withdraw only the shortfall when your available
  balance does not cover them, in the same signed batch as the transfer.

Aave lets a supplier withdraw only what the market has not lent out. When
utilisation is near 100%, a withdrawal can wait until borrowers repay or the
rate draws in new supply. The 25 to 30% buffer and the pause threshold exist so
that ordinary refunds and withdrawals do not depend on that. When your
available balance falls under the buffer while the market is past the pause
threshold, Keycat opens a liquidity alert on the savings screen and pushes it
to your devices.

## Turning savings off

Turning savings off is one signed batch: `disableModule` removes the modifier
from your Safe, and `USDC.approve(Pool, 0)` clears any allowance left. The
saved balance stays in Aave, earning, until you withdraw it. A turned-off
modifier can be enabled again later; it keeps its address.

## Earnings

Your savings balance is your Safe's aUSDC balance, read from Base. Earnings are
that balance above your principal: everything the operator supplied, less
everything an owner withdrew. A withdrawal counts fully against principal, so
after one, earnings are understated rather than overstated. Keycat shows
earnings after the fact and never advertises a rate.

<Callout type="warning">
  Lending carries risk. A defect in Aave's contracts, or a market that stays
  fully borrowed, could delay or reduce what comes back. Keep in savings only
  what you can leave there, and lower the share or turn savings off if that
  changes.
</Callout>
