No server? Your [payment page](/guides/payment-pages) and payment links take
payments with no code. The [drop-in button](/guides/drop-in-button) adds a pay
button to any web page with one script tag. For anything that needs to know an
order was paid, use [the API](/quickstart) and [webhooks](/webhooks).
