# Moving from Coinbase Commerce

<Steps>

### Import your history

Export your charges from Coinbase Commerce as CSV. In Keycat, open your
business's **Settings → Import your Commerce history** and upload the file.

Keycat reads each row's id, status, amount, currency, description, and date,
matching Commerce's usual column names. Imported records are history only: they
never create a charge or a refund, and never move money. Importing the same file
again skips rows already imported.

### Switch your integration

Replace Commerce's payment links with your [payment page](/guides/payment-pages)
or reusable charges. Replace API-created charges with
[`createCharge`](/quickstart). The concepts map closely:

| Coinbase Commerce | Keycat |
| --- | --- |
| Charge | [Charge](/charges) |
| Checkout | Reusable charge |
| `charge:confirmed` | `charge.paid` |
| `charge:failed` (expired) | `charge.expired` |
| `X-CC-Webhook-Signature` | `X-Keycat-Signature`, [timestamped](/webhooks#verifying-signatures) |

### Test, then turn off the old one

Send a small payment to yourself through the new integration and confirm your
webhook handler sees it. Run both side by side for as long as you like before
removing Commerce.

</Steps>
